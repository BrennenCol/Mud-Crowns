# App Store promo pictures

BitLife-style pictures that show everyone you can be: a serf, a milkmaid, a knight, an abbess, an outlaw, a merchant, a king or a queen. Each picture is the real game with a bold caption and a gold medallion of the person it's about. Picture 4 follows Hob Thatcher from swineherd to king; picture 6 is his gravestone.

## Product page header: 3840 × 1646 (21:9)

`MudCrowns_header_3840x1646.png` is the wide banner at the top of the product page, like BitLife's: the name in gold on sun rays, a gold ribbon ("Serf or sovereign: who will you be?"), and seven different people in gold medallions (serf, milkmaid, knight, king, queen, abbess, outlaw). The name and ribbon sit inside Apple's central safe area (1646 × 661 at x 1097, y 493); the medallions fill the width a phone shows. No transparency. `preview/phone_preview_header.png` is a mock-up of how it looks on a phone, for checking only: do not upload it.

Headers are new with iOS 27 and only show on iOS 27 or later. Upload: your app, the version, Product Page Information, Header and Search Results, Header. Drag the file in, Save, then submit the version for review; it goes live when that version is approved and released. Use Preview there to see how it looks.
