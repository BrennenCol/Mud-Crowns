# App Store promo pictures

BitLife-style pictures that say what the game is: a medieval life simulator where you can rise from serf to king, and be anyone along the way. Each picture is the real game with a bold caption and gold medallions of the people it's about. The lowest rank is called a serf, the game's own word (bound to the lord's land), rather than "slave".

## Product page header: 3840 × 1646 (21:9)

`MudCrowns_header_3840x1646.png` is the wide banner at the top of the product page, like BitLife's: one life walking left to right, painted by the game's own scene painter. A baby sitting up and waving in a wicker basket by a thatched hovel, a serf boy with the lord's pink pig, a peasant with a broad-bladed hoe, a craftsman in a full apron with his hammer raised, a merchant with his purse, a knight in a white surcoat with a red cross, his shield on his arm and a spear with a red pennon, a lord with his hound sitting at his side, and a king in crown and ermine on the red carpet before his castle. The road goes from mud to cobbles to carpet. Over each stage is an icon in a parchment badge with the game's gold marker; the name is in the sky, and a ribbon along the bottom reads "A medieval life simulator: rise from serf to king". The name sits inside Apple's central safe area (1646 × 661 at x 1097, y 493). No transparency. `preview/phone_preview_header.png` is a mock-up of how it looks on a phone, for checking only: do not upload it.
