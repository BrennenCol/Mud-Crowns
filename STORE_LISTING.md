## Description (both stores)

```
Live a whole life in a medieval kingdom, one year at a time.

Be born a serf in a muddy village, a smith's child, a merchant's heir or a royal prince. Each year you have three deeds: work your trade, court a sweetheart, pray, train, scheme, feast or march to war. Then turn the page and see what the year brings.

A WORLD THAT LIVES WITHOUT YOU
Kings age, marry and die. Heirs fight over empty thrones. Plague and famine sweep the counties, outlaws and knights make their names, and your neighbours have lives of their own. The chronicle tells you all of it.

NEARLY 300 POSTS TO RISE THROUGH
Farm labourer, apprentice, blacksmith, apothecary, monk, minstrel, thief, sailor, knight, merchant, royal guard, chancellor, pirate queen. Careers have real steps: learn the work in one post to earn the next, or find a way around it through noble birth, the king's favour or a powerful patron.

SHIPS, TRADE AND HIGH OFFICE
Captain a pirate ship and raid the coasts. Send trade runs across six markets. Serve the crown with duties of your own.

WAR AND CONQUEST
Raise an army, choose your tactics and take counties on a living map of nine kingdoms. Rule as a lord, then as a monarch with a council, taxes and decrees.

LOVE, FAMILY AND DYNASTY
Court, marry and raise children. When you die, carry on as your heir. Your graveyard remembers every life.

FAIR AND PRIVATE
No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Plays fully offline. Your saves stay on your device, and a backup code moves them to a new phone.
```

```
THE HALL OF LEGENDS
Every life is now weighed by its Rise: how far you climbed from the rung you were born on. Each step up Fortune's ladder is worth more than the last, and the humbler your birth, the more the whole climb counts. A serf who wins a crown outshines any prince born to one. Your greatest lives stand on a gold, silver and bronze podium, and your family houses add up their glory, generation after generation.

THE COLLECTION HALL
Sixteen relics, Rare, Epic, Legendary and Mythic, and the only way to win them is to climb from a humble birth: the Broken Fetter, the Merchant's Scales, the Golden Spurs, the Count's Banner, the Great Seal, the Keys of Heaven, the Ploughman's Crown, the Orb of the World and more. Each one stands in its own gilded niche under a beam of light; the ones you have not won wait behind iron bars and a padlock. Win one and it rises out of the dark in a burst of light and sparks.

THE PATH OF GLORY
Rewards on the way: at 1 relic the Hall's candles are lit, at 4 a bronze crest crowns your main menu, at 8 your coat of arms is framed in gold in every life, at 12 a jewelled crest. Finish a wing and its banner hangs over your main menu, and its window in the Hall lights up. Fill every case, and the sealed reliquary opens...

LAND FOR SALE
Rich at last, but no land? Land was almost never sold, so wait for a ruined knight, a widow or a king who needs money. A manor costs about twenty years of its rents, plus the king's licence and the lawyers, and makes you Lord or Lady of it. Lend silver against a manor and take it if the debt goes bad. When the crown is short of money for a war, buy a whole county.

SMOOTH AND STEADY
The Hall scrolls smoothly on every phone, and when you win a relic its card holds still and fits your screen.

FORTUNE'S LADDER
See where you stand, how far you have climbed and what the next rung is worth, in the Menu and in Find Work.

ALSO NEW SINCE 1.13
```

Versions 1.5.1 to 1.8 rate about **13+** on the App Store. From 1.9, with

Store and about **PEGI 16** on Google Play. On Google Play's target audience
question, choose **16 and over** from 1.9, so the "Designed for Families" rules
don't apply.
