## Description (both stores)

```
Live a whole life in a medieval kingdom, one year at a time.

Be born a serf in a muddy village, a smith's child, a merchant's heir or a royal prince. Each year you have three deeds: work your trade, court a sweetheart, pray, train, scheme, feast or march to war. Then turn the page and see what the year brings.

A WORLD THAT LIVES WITHOUT YOU
Kings age, marry and die. Heirs fight over empty thrones. Plague and famine sweep the counties, outlaws and knights make their names, and your neighbours have lives of their own. The chronicle tells you all of it.

NEARLY 300 POSTS TO RISE THROUGH
Farm labourer, apprentice, blacksmith, apothecary, monk, minstrel, thief, sailor, knight, merchant, royal guard, chancellor, pirate queen. Careers have real steps: learn the work in one post to earn the next, or find a way around it through noble birth, the king's favour or a powerful patron.

SHIPS, TRADE AND HIGH OFFICE
Captain a pirate ship and raid the coasts. Send trade runs across six markets. Serve the crown with duties of your own.

WAR AND CONQUEST
Raise an army, choose your tactics and take counties on a living map of nine kingdoms. Rule as a lord, then as a monarch with a council, taxes and decrees.

LOVE, FAMILY AND DYNASTY
Court, marry and raise children. When you die, carry on as your heir. Your graveyard remembers every life.

FAIR AND PRIVATE
No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Plays fully offline. Your saves stay on your device, and a backup code moves them to a new phone.
```

## What's new (for this version)

Also in `store/whats_new_1.18.1.txt`, ready to copy (it includes 1.17 and ends with a line about the updates since 1.13).

```
CHOICES THAT COME BACK
Small choices at work are remembered. Lend your neighbour your ox, take a soldier's sword for his drinking debt, rush a rich customer's order, keep a purse you found on a dead knight, warn a kind steward of a plot. A year or three later the consequence turns up at your door, with a reminder of what you did, and another choice to make. There are 26 of these, across every kind of work, from the fields and the bakehouse to the Church, the army and the sea.

FIXED: GREYING HAIR
Portraits no longer flicker between grey and their old colour as people age. Hair now greys slowly, starting at a different age for everyone, and turns white in old age.

SHORTER, SHARPER
The long storylines that came back chapter by chapter are gone. Your own work and your own choices tell the story now.

YOUR OFFICE, PLAYED
High posts now come with real work you play yourself, once a year, without spending a deed. The better you play, the more silver, Renown and standing it brings:
- Hear the petitions: three petitioners, two choices each, and four great powers to keep in balance
- The Exchequer: count the sheriffs' tally sticks and catch the cheats
- The assize: weigh the evidence, spot the lie, and give your verdict (and maybe a bribe)
- The war table: set your archers, pikes and horse against the enemy, read the ground and the weather, and take knights and lords for ransom
- The visitation: find the rotten priests and protect the good ones
- The sickroom: read the signs and choose the cure, from a carter's wife up to the king
- The great work: raise a bridge, a tower or a cathedral over the years, and survive the floods and cracks
- The envoy's game: find out what a foreign king wants, and win a treaty without giving too much away
- Corner the market: venture your own silver over four fairs
- The spymaster's web: read the whispers and unmask the traitor at court

THE FOUR POWERS
Crown, Church, Lords and Commons each watch what you do. Too low and they turn on you; too high and the others grow jealous.

A RIVAL AT COURT
Someone wants your post. Beat them in a battle of wits each year; break them three times and they are ruined.

THE CONCLAVE
As a cardinal, win the papal election yourself, one vote at a time.

LAURELS
Every win earns laurels. Rise from Untried to Able, Renowned and Legendary, with rewards up to two and a half times bigger, and a seal of fame at the top.

ALSO NEW SINCE 1.13
The Hall of Legends and the Collection Hall, land for sale, new music, workplaces you can see, and better pictures.
```

Versions 1.5.1 to 1.8 rate about **13+** on the App Store. From 1.9, with

Store and about **PEGI 16** on Google Play. On Google Play's target audience
question, choose **16 and over** from 1.9, so the "Designed for Families" rules
don't apply.
