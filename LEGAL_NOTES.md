# Staying clear of other life-sim games

*This is general information, not legal advice. Before you sell the game, have
an intellectual-property lawyer look it over, especially the name and the store
listing.*

## What's protected and what isn't

- **Game ideas and rules aren't owned by anyone.** "Live a life year by year,
  make choices, see random events" is a genre, like "match three" or "tower
  defense". Many life simulators exist, and copyright doesn't cover mechanics.
- **What *is* protected:**
  - another game's **name and logo** (trademark)
  - its **exact text, art, icons, sounds and code** (copyright)
  - its distinctive **look and feel** (trade dress)
  - anything that makes buyers think your game *is* theirs or comes from the
    same company

## What this game already does right

- An original name, art style (illuminated manuscript, wax seal, parchment),
  writing, and code. No other game's text, images, or code was used.
- Its own systems: over a hundred historical occupations with experience and
  career roads, social class and Influence, the hex map of seven kingdoms with
  conquest, dynasties across generations, historical gender rules, seals of
  fame, and ambitions.
- The other game's name appears nowhere in the game.

## Rules to keep following

1. **Never use another game's name** in your title, subtitle, keywords, ads, or
   description. Don't write "like BitLife" or "medieval BitLife", and don't
   bid on their name in ads. This is the single most common way small
   developers get takedown notices.
2. **Don't copy their screens.** Keep your own layout, colours, icon style and
   button design. Don't make your icon look like theirs.
3. **Don't copy their wording** for events, achievements, or menus. Write your
   own (as this game does).
4. **Check your name.** Search "Mud & Crowns" in the
   [USPTO trademark search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov) and in both app stores
   before launch. Consider registering it once the game earns money.
5. **Keep records.** Your git history shows the game was built independently,
   which is useful if anyone ever asks.

## Fonts and other assets

- EB Garamond and UnifrakturMaguntia are under the SIL Open Font License. They
  are free to bundle and sell inside an app. Keep the license note in
  `fonts/fonts.css`.
- The game's pictures are system emoji. They are drawn by the phone itself, so
  nothing needs licensing.
- The app icon in `icons/` was made for this game.
