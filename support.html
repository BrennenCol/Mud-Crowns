Mud & Crowns

Help and support

Found a bug, or have an idea? Tell us on the game’s GitHub page. Say what you were doing, your phone, and the version number from the menu.

Common questions

Where is my game saved?
On your device only. It saves at the end of every year.

How do I move my game to a new phone?
Open the menu and choose Back up or move your game. Copy the code, then paste it into Mud & Crowns on the new phone.

How do I play?
Each year you have three deeds. Spend them in Work, Deeds, Kin or Realm, then tap Turn the page. The menu has a guide and can replay the welcome tour.

Something went wrong.
If the game ever breaks, it saves and offers to carry on. If a save is damaged, the year before is restored automatically.

Privacy policy: no data is collected.
