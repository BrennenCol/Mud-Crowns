Mud & Crowns

Privacy policy
Last updated October 2026

Mud & Crowns does not collect, store or share any personal information.

  • Your game saves, graveyard, settings and achievements are stored only on your own device, in the app’s local storage. They never leave your device.
  • The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • The game does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or any other device data.
  • The iPhone and Android apps carry everything they need and make no network requests. The web version loads its typefaces from Google Fonts.
  • A backup code (Menu, Back up or move your game) is made on your device and goes only where you paste it.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

If this ever changes, this policy will be updated before the change ships.

Questions: open an issue on the game’s GitHub page.
